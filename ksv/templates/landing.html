About Organ Connect

Organ Connect is an advanced AI-assisted organ donation registry and matching platform designed to eliminate waiting list biases and streamline clinical verification.

Rather than relying on manual data entry, Organ Connect empowers donors and receivers to upload authentic medical documents (blood reports, HLA tissue typing, doctor diagnosis, and fitness certificates). Approved Government Hospitals audit these documents, verify legitimacy, and supply clinical sign-off remarks.

✓ Multi-Factor AI Engine: Matches based on Organ, ABO/Rh Blood Compatibility, HLA Tissue Alignment, Weight Ratio, and Urgency.
✓ Strict Admin Approval: Hospitals must be authorized by System Administrators before accessing patient files.
